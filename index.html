§1About

I go by 3nvariable. The name is a small joke about systems that never quite resolve: add one more unknown and the arithmetic still has to close. By day I operate compute — machines that multiply matrices until the heat sinks sing — and by night I write down what that did to me. This page is the drawer where those notes live.

n has been pending resolution since registration.

I like small programs, short proofs, and long walks between the two. For now this manuscript is a proof mass: filler holding the exact shape of the real thing. When the writing is ready, it will land in w without ceremony, and the revision date above will tick forward by one.
