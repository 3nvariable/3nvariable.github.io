Solve for n

filed 04 jul 2026 · 4 min · all writings

A pseudonym is a variable declaration. You choose a name that binds to nothing, introduce it into evidence, and let the system try to resolve it. Most readers substitute a value anyway — a face, an age, a grudge. The point of the exercise is that the substitution fails.

I am called 3nvariable, which parses as three, n-variable or as three-n, variable, and I have stopped choosing between them. A name that resolves two ways cannot be resolved one way. That is not evasiveness. That is correct behavior for a name that contains an unknown.

n ∼ N(0, σ²) (3)
the mean is zero on purpose.

Three variables hold this site up: what I write, what I build, and where I answer mail. Everything else — the biography, the affiliation, the photograph — belongs to the error term. The manuscript is cleaner without it. The arithmetic closes.

If you are sure you have solved for me somewhere else, keep it to yourself. A proof nobody checks is called a belief, and this page is already full of those.
